# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

![Retail Sales Performance Dashboard](Retail_Sales_Performance_Analysis.gif)

## Introduction

This project analyzes retail sales data from 2024 and 2025 to evaluate sales, profitability, products, customers, promotions, and returns.

The goal was to identify the main factors affecting business performance and present the findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## Technical Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Power Pivot
- DAX
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers

## Dataset

The dataset contains retail transaction data from 2024 and 2025, including products, customers, sales representatives, promotions, sales channels, and returns.

The data was cleaned using Power Query and modeled in Power Pivot using Fact and Dimension tables.

# Analysis

## 1. Did sales growth lead to higher profitability?

**Tools:** Power Pivot, DAX, Pivot Tables

**Insight:**  
Net Sales increased by **3.36%**, while Profit increased by only **1.13%**. Profit Margin declined from **27.46% to 26.87%**.

## 2. What affected the decline in Profit Margin?

**Tools:** DAX, Pivot Tables

**Insight:**  
Product Cost Rate increased from **71.94% to 72.53%**, while discounts and returns also increased.

## 3. Which sales channels performed best?

**Tools:** Pivot Tables, DAX, Pivot Charts

**Insight:**  
Corporate Sales generated the highest Net Sales at approximately **8.26M SAR**, while Retail Store achieved the highest Profit Margin at **31.42%**.

## 4. Which products contributed to declining profitability?

**Tools:** Power Pivot, DAX, Pivot Tables

**Insight:**  
Apex Notebook A112 was the largest contributor to the decline in laptop profitability, with profit decreasing by approximately **105.3K SAR**.

## 5. Where were returns increasing?

**Tools:** DAX, Pivot Tables

**Insight:**  
Footwear Return Rate increased from **8.79% to 14.57%**.

## 6. Were high-sales promotions also profitable?

**Tools:** Pivot Tables, DAX, Pivot Charts

**Insight:**  
Ramadan Savings generated the highest promotional sales, while Back to School achieved a stronger Profit Margin.

## 7. Who were the strongest sales representatives?

**Tools:** Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts

**Insight:**  
M. Alqahtani ranked first with approximately **3.92M SAR** in 2025 Net Sales.

## 8. How did customer segments perform?

**Tools:** Power Pivot, DAX, Pivot Tables

**Insight:**  
Corporate customers generated higher sales per customer, while Consumers had a larger customer base, higher Profit Margin, and lower Return Rate.

# Dashboard

## Charts

**Used:** Pivot Charts and bar/trend charts.

**Why:**  
To compare sales channels, sales representatives, promotions, and monthly performance.

**Result:**  
The charts make the main business trends and comparisons easier to understand.

## Calculations

**Used:** DAX measures and Power Pivot.

**Why:**  
To calculate KPIs such as Net Sales, Profit, Profit Margin, Annual Sales Growth, Orders, and Return Rate.

**Result:**  
The KPIs update automatically when dashboard filters are changed.

## Data Validation

**Used:** Power Query.

**Why:**  
To check dates, quantities, discounts, ratings, missing values, and text consistency before analysis.

**Result:**  
The data was cleaned and prepared for reliable analysis.

## Interactivity

Slicers were used to filter the dashboard by:

- Year
- Sales Channel
- Region
- Category
